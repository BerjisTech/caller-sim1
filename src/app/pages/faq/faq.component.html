Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about nichapie.com

What is Nichapie?

Nichapie is a platform where professionals can connect briefly through video calls to discuss work-related topics, share ideas, or seek advice anonymously.

Is the platform free?

Yes! The platform is free to use. We may offer premium features in the future to enhance the experience.

How does professional matching work?

Our platform uses advanced algorithms to match professionals based on industry, expertise, and goals. You'll be connected with relevant professionals for meaningful conversations and potential collaborations.

How do I join a conversation?

You can join a conversation by creating a room, joining a random room based on tags, or using a join link for an active room.

What topics can I discuss?

You can discuss any work-related topics, such as solving bugs, career advice, or even general workplace experiences, using relevant tags.

Are my conversations secure and private?

Yes! All conversations are encrypted and no personal data is stored. Shared notes, jamboards, and screens are deleted once the call ends.

How is my privacy protected?

We prioritize your privacy and security. All conversations are encrypted, and you control what information is visible on your profile. You can choose to remain anonymous until you're comfortable sharing more details.

What makes this platform different from LinkedIn?

Unlike LinkedIn, our platform focuses on real-time, meaningful connections. We facilitate direct, one-on-one conversations with professionals in your field, making networking more personal and productive.

How can I report inappropriate behavior?

We have a zero-tolerance policy for inappropriate behavior. You can report any concerns using the "Report" summary during or after conversations. Our team reviews all reports within 24 hours and takes appropriate action.
